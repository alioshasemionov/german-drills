VerbCombo

Funktionsverbgefüge — fixed noun-verb collocations

German often expresses an action as a noun paired with a semantically bleached "support" verb, rather than as one simple verb — and only one specific verb combines with each noun. This drill shows a noun phrase and asks you to supply the one functional verb that fits it, plus its plain single-verb equivalent.

eine Entscheidung treffen = entscheiden Rücksicht nehmen = rücksichtsvoll sein in Kraft treten = gelten
