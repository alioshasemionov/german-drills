German Drills

Small, single-page browser games for drilling German grammar. No accounts, no build step — each game is one self-contained HTML file.

Stammformen

Drill the Präteritum, Partizip II, and auxiliary (hat/ist) of 388 German verbs — regular and irregular, evenly balanced. Typed answers, an "Ich weiß es nicht" reveal, and per-session verb filters.

388 verbs · play →
Verbnester

Match prefixes to meanings across 50 verb "nests" (steigen → einsteigen/aussteigen/umsteigen/…) — tap-to-place or drag-and-drop, with decoy prefixes so the last row can't be guessed by elimination.
